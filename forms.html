Forms

Everything you might be asked to bring signed

Medical forms, applications, permission slips and the troop's own gear paperwork. If you are new, the two that matter are the youth application and parts A and B of the medical record.

Medical forms expire annually. Parts A and B every year for everyone. Part C, signed by a physician, for anything longer than 72 hours — which means summer camp. Get it done in the spring, not in June.
