How this works

Changes are staged in your browser first. Nothing is public until you press Publish in the bar at the bottom — and the bar tells you exactly which files will change.

Publishing writes the JSON files in the site's repository and the live site updates within a minute or two. If you have made a mess, do not panic: every save is a commit, and the previous version can be restored.

Rules the editor enforces

  • If the current content could not be loaded, saving is blocked. A failed read followed by a save is how sites get wiped.
  • If a save would delete a large part of a file, you have to confirm it twice — once here and once on the server.
  • Youth are listed by first name and last initial. Do not paste full names, phone numbers or addresses into any field.
