The troop runs on its patrols

A patrol is six to eight Scouts who camp, cook, and compete together. Patrols elect their own leaders, plan their own menus, and are how nearly everything in Troop 3 actually gets done.

How the patrol method works here

New Scouts usually join a patrol within their first few weeks. On a campout your patrol plans the menu, buys the food, cooks it, and cleans up after itself — adults do not do this for you. Patrol leaders run their own patrol meetings inside the Tuesday night meeting.

Every patrol competes for the National Honor Patrol award. It takes a real year's worth of camping, service and meetings; it is not handed out.
